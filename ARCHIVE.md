# Selected Work — Paper Drawer v1

Archived on 2026-09-04 as a preserved interaction study.

This is the earlier, ceremonial version of the Selected Work TL;DR interaction:

- Each project visual remains full-bleed inside a fixed-height card.
- A folder-shaped paper surface rests over the lower part of the visual.
- Hover lifts the card and nudges the paper drawer upward.
- Selecting `TL;DR` slides the drawer over the visual and reveals a 30-second summary.
- The title view and summary view cross-fade inside the same drawer.
- Only one project summary can be open at a time; `Escape` closes it.

It was not selected as the final direction, but it is intentionally preserved for its more graphic, ritual-like character.
